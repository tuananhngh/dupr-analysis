Get your report
  1. Once: drag this button to your bookmarks bar (show the bar with ⌘⇧B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows)
    ⟳ Sync DUPR
  2. Open dashboard.dupr.com and log in as usual.
  3. Click the ⟳ Sync DUPR bookmark. Your matches are downloaded and you come back here to your report.

You log in on DUPR's own site: your password never goes through this page. Your data stays in your browser. It is never uploaded anywhere. Works on a computer (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
